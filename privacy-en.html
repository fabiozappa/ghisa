Privacy notice

Effective 9 September 2026

In short: we ask for no email and no personal details. We collect the minimum needed to run the service. You can delete everything yourself, from the app, at any time. For the same reason, credentials cannot be recovered: lose them and your data becomes unreachable.

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

1. Who processes the data

Data controller: to be provided. Contact: to be provided.

2. What we collect

  • Username: the one you choose. It need not contain your real name, and you are better off if it does not.
  • Password verifier: the two words are not stored. We keep only a cryptographic hash, from which the original words cannot be derived.
  • What you type into the app: workouts, exercises, targets, rest times, any links you add, and your training history (dates, sets, weights, reps, notes).
  • IP address, together with the username and a failure count, only when a login fails. It is used to slow down password guessing, and is deleted automatically after 15 minutes.
  • A session cookie, strictly necessary to keep you signed in. No profiling cookies.

The hosting provider may additionally record server accesses (IP address, date, requested page) in its own technical logs, under its own policies.

3. What we do NOT collect

No email, no phone number, no real name, no date of birth, no location, no payment details. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contacts no external service: there are no remote fonts, CDNs or analytics tools.

Weights and reps are typed by you and stay yours: they are not used to profile you and are not combined with anything else.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the service you asked for by creating an account (legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To protect sign-in against automated attempts — the only use of the IP address (legal basis: legitimate interest in security, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and workouts: until you delete your account.
  • Workouts and exercises you delete: kept in a bin for 30 days, then permanently removed.
  • Failed login attempts (IP): 15 minutes.
  • Backups: database backups may still contain your data for to be provided after deletion, until rotation overwrites them.

6. Credentials cannot be recovered

This follows directly from not asking for your email. There is no "forgot password", because there is no channel through which to verify that it is really you.

If you forget your username or your two words, nobody can restore access to the account, not even whoever runs the service: your data remains but becomes unreachable. Write your credentials down somewhere safe.

7. If you share a workout

Sharing is never automatic: it happens only if you press the button yourself. Doing so generates a secret link, and anyone holding that link can see the workout name and its exercises, with no account needed. Treat it like a password.

The shared page never shows your training history, the weights you lift, your username or any other data about you.

You can revoke sharing at any time and the link stops working. Note that anyone who already imported the workout keeps their own copy, which remains theirs even after revocation or after you delete your account.

8. Deleting everything

You can delete your account yourself, from the app: Account → Delete account. You will be asked for your two words as confirmation.

Deletion is immediate, total and irreversible: the user, the workouts, the exercises and the whole training history are removed. We keep no copy, except as stated in section 5 about backups. No recovery is possible, not even on request.

9. Your rights

You may exercise the rights under Articles 15-22 GDPR. In practice:

  • Access: all data about you is visible in the app; there is none held anywhere else.
  • Rectification: edit workouts, exercises and names directly in the app.
  • Erasure: see section 8 — you do it yourself, immediately.
  • Portability: you can copy each workout summary as text, and share a workout via link.
  • Complaint: you may lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (www.garanteprivacy.it) or your local authority.

Since we collect no identifying data, a request sent from outside cannot be verified: this is why the tools live inside the app, where you are already authenticated.

10. Who we share data with

Nobody. Data is not sold, transferred or shared with third parties. It stays on the server hosting the service, operated by the hosting provider acting as data processor.

11. Changes

This notice may change. The version published on this page is the only one in force.
